## LinkedIn

I’ve been working on BloomAir, an open-source AirPlay receiver for Android TV.

The goal is to make screen mirroring simple: open BloomAir on the TV, select “BloomAir TV” from Screen Mirroring on a Mac or iPad, and watch on the larger display. I tested the connection on a real Android TV and improved the video decoder’s handling of temporary hardware backpressure.

The project includes the Android app, setup instructions, device-testing notes, and deployment scripts.
